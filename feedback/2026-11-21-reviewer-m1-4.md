## App defects

- **Duplicate reminder rows:** a book, cancel and rebook gives two rows, both with id
  `reminder-11`. Nothing warned about the duplicate id.
- **Staff can book.**

## tinyjump problems

- **"Past" shows late:** it appears only on the next minute's redraw, so a slot whose start
  has seconds in it stays bookable on screen for 9 to 25 s.
- **`<Input type=datetime>`:**
  - it shows the browser's locale format, not the spec's `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`;
  - its button label says "local" even with `zone="UTC"`.
- **No offline shell on the web:**
  - a reload while offline fails;
  - there is no pending state;
  - an old error stays on screen.
- **Chrome's offline emulation** does not cut the event stream.
- **`tj review` against `tj serve` says "no screen".** Each role needs its own
  `tj dev --as` restart.
- **Element ids:** they are `data-tj-id`, not DOM `id`.
- **`Date.now()`** returns fractional milliseconds.
