## Failures & Partial Passes

**B18 Offline (2/3) — second offline action silently dropped**
- **Steps:** Sign in as customer (`alice`) with booked slot `off1` and open slot `off2`. Go offline (Chrome CDP network offline). Press `book-off2`, then press `cancel-off1`. Go back online.
- **Expected:** Both queued actions execute on reconnect (`off2` booked, `off1` open).
- **Actual:** `off2` booked, `off1` stayed booked (`cancel-off1` dropped). `App()` shares one `Form.get("book")` across all book/cancel/remove buttons (`app.tj:266,277-280`); while the first offline promise waits, `book.busy()` stays `true`, disabling other `book-*` buttons and silently ignoring `cancel-*` presses.
- **Cause:** App (reusing single-flight `Form` for list actions) + tinyjump (`tj:ui` lacks an outbox-aware per-action helper).

## App Defects

- **Access rules wider than needed:** `Customer.reminders` (`app.tj:60`) lacks `@read("owner")`, so staff reads customers' private reminders via `GET /api/Customer/<id>`. `Slot.book` (`app.tj:109`) uses `@allow("signedIn")`, letting staff save a profile and book via HTTP API (`200 OK`), while UI hides booking from staff.
- **Ghost-booked slot on CLI/retention erasure:** `Slot` duplicates `booked: boolean` alongside `customer: string` (`app.tj:91-92`). Running `tj privacy erase key:bob` clears `Slot/s_chk#customer` to `""` but leaves `{"booked":true,"customer":""}`, stranding the future slot booked with no customer.
- **Minor:** `validSlot` and `validReminder` accept fractional minutes (`30.5` returns `200 OK`). Already-delivered reminders remain in `reminders` if the slot is later cancelled.

## Tinyjump Problems

- **Broken doc advice vs `@read("staff", "self")`:** `docs/language.md:173` says *"Let `customer` alone say whether a slot is booked (`s.customer !== ""`)"*, which breaks whenever other customers must see `"booked"` (they receive `""`). Without computed public getters or presence masking, apps must duplicate `booked: boolean` and break under `tj privacy erase`.
- **Flow check contradicts docs & pollutes Art. 30 record:** `docs/language.md:235` says device-local objects may hold PII, yet `Screen.showMyData` had to wrap `exportData(caller.id)` in `reveal(data, "booking")` (`app.tj:171`), making `tj check --privacy --record` falsely list `app.tj:171` under `"Where it is let out"`. That record also prints `Lawful basis for each purpose: ____` despite `const lawfulBasis` being declared.
- **Missing visibility/role primitives:** No server-only/private field rule (forcing `@read("staff")` on internal `Slot.alarm`), and no role exclusion in `@allow` (`signedIn` without `staff`).
- **Server `check()` drops `field`:** Because wire refusals omit `e.field`, apps duplicate validation calls client-side to show field errors.
- **Doc mismatch:** `GET /api/whoami` returns `{"ok":{"id":...,"roles":[...],"dev":false}}`, not `{ id, roles }` (`docs/access-control.md:241`).
