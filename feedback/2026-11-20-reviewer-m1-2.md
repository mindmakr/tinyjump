## B12 fails: one customer's data is shown to the next customer on a shared browser

1. Cara pressed `my-data`, signed out, and dan signed in in the same window.
2. Dan's `my-data-view` showed cara's whole export: her name, phone, bookings and
   reminders.
3. It was still there after a reload (`tj:Screen/main` in storage), and after cara deleted
   her account.

**Cause:**
- **The app:** it keeps per-person screen state in the device-local
  `Screen.get("main")`.
- **tinyjump:** sign-out cleared replicas and the outbox, but not device-local objects,
  and the docs did not warn about it.
  - **Fixed since, on `m1-fixes-1`** (visionary-005, decision 2): a signed-in user
    leaving resets the device's own objects, in memory and in storage.
